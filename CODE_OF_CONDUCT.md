# Code of Conduct

QTP aims to be a practical, respectful open-source project for people building and testing AI applications.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, especially when someone is reporting a confusing bug.
- Discuss ideas through evidence: reproduction steps, screenshots, logs, tests, and docs.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Repeated off-topic comments that derail project work.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Bad-faith issue spam or intentionally misleading reports.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community. If you see a problem, report it through the support channels in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md).
